# Evaluation Test Briefs

## Brief 001: Product Launch Announcement (Clean)
- **ID:** brief-001
- **Content Type:** product_launch
- **Topic:** Official launch announcement for the Aurora X3 smartphone.
- **Constraints:** Confident, forward-looking tone highlighting everyday usability, triple-lens low-light photography, and all-day battery performance. Avoid spec dumping and banned superlatives. Include a clear CTA to visit a retail store or order online.
- **Target Platform:** Website landing page + press release

## Brief 002: Feature Announcement (Clean)
- **ID:** brief-002
- **Content Type:** feature_announcement
- **Topic:** Feature announcement spotlighting Night Mode on Aurora X3.
- **Constraints:** Single-feature focus using problem/solution (before/after) framing: contrast past blurry, grainy low-light shots with automatic true-color night photography. Keep length under 150 words. Emphasize that it is built-in with no extra purchase required.
- **Target Platform:** Product blog + social feature card

## Brief 003: Product Campaign (Clean)
- **ID:** brief-003
- **Content Type:** product_campaign
- **Topic:** Cross-channel product campaign centered on upgrading to Aurora X3.
- **Constraints:** Establish the core campaign tagline "Trade up, not down." Frame the message around daily customer benefits (easy credit, real-world battery life, smooth performance) rather than lab benchmarks. Ensure copy structure can adapt across email, social, and in-store displays.
- **Target Platform:** Multi-channel (email, social, retail signage)

## Brief 004: Promotional Email (Clean)
- **ID:** brief-004
- **Content Type:** promotional_email
- **Topic:** Limited-time trade-in promotional email for Aurora X3.
- **Constraints:** Subject line strictly under 50 characters. Include preview text and focus on instant trade-in credit at checkout and secure data transfer. Include a single, benefit-led CTA button line. Do not use exaggerated scarcity or banned urgency language.
- **Target Platform:** Email

## Brief 005: Software Update (Clean)
- **ID:** brief-005
- **Content Type:** software_update
- **Topic:** OS 9 software update release notes.
- **Constraints:** Lead with the core customer benefits (longer battery life via background activity management, faster low-light Face Unlock, redesigned notification summary) rather than dense changelog specs. State that the update is free and provide clear navigation to Settings > Software Update.
- **Target Platform:** In-product notification + support changelog

## Brief 006: Brand Campaign (Clean)
- **ID:** brief-006
- **Content Type:** brand_campaign
- **Topic:** Brand campaign: "Designed for the Way You Live."
- **Constraints:** Focus entirely on human-centric technology and real everyday moments (screen dimming at bedtime, low-light family dinners). Strictly avoid hardware specs or technical jargon. Clearly convey the core brand pillars: "Approachable" and "Premium but not elitist."
- **Target Platform:** Brand portal + print campaign

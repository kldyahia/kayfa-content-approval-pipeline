# What's New: OS 9

OS 9 is rolling out now, and the headline change is simple: your phone lasts longer between charges. A smarter background-activity manager keeps the apps you check often fast, while the ones you rarely open stop draining power in the background.

Also in this update: faster Face Unlock in low light, and a redesigned notification summary so your lock screen shows what actually matters first.

Update from Settings > Software Update. It's free, and it takes about ten minutes.

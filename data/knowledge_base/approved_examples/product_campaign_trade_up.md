# Campaign: "Trade Up, Not Down"

**Tagline:** Trade up, not down.

Your old phone got you this far. Aurora X3 gets you further — same easy trade-in credit, a smoother everyday experience, and a battery that keeps pace with your actual day, not a lab test.

**Channel notes:** The tagline should appear consistently across email, social, and in-store signage. Body copy can flex in length by channel, but the benefit ladder — trade-in ease, then daily performance, then battery — stays the same.

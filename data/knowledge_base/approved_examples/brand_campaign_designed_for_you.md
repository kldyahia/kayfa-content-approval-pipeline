# Campaign: "Designed for the Way You Live"

Some technology asks you to adapt to it. We think it should be the other way around.

Every choice we make — how a screen dims at bedtime, how a camera behaves in a dim restaurant, how a battery holds up on your longest day — starts with a real moment in a real life, not a spec sheet.

That's the whole idea behind everything we build: premium doesn't have to mean complicated, and confident doesn't have to mean cold.

**Brand pillars expressed:** Approachable, Premium but not elitist.

# New: Night Mode on Aurora X3

Low light used to mean grainy, blurry photos you'd delete before anyone saw them. Night Mode changes that.

Point, tap, and Aurora X3 automatically brightens the scene while keeping colors true — no tripod, no settings menu, no guesswork. Your dinner table, your city walk, your kid's birthday candles: all camera-ready, even after the sun goes down.

Night Mode is available now on every Aurora X3, no extra purchase required.
